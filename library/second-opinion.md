# Cross-model second opinion

Get a second opinion from the other model family. From Claude Code, consult Astra through Codex; from Codex, consult Fable through Claude Code, unless I have specified another model. Make a real separate call rather than playing both roles yourself. Use the existing authenticated tools and check their available commands and model names as needed. Report any substitution or access failure.

Give the reviewer the original question, relevant constraints, and the material needed to assess the work. Include the relevant code, results, derivations, or sources rather than just your account of them. Do not ask it to confirm your preferred explanation. For a difficult conceptual question, an independent approach before seeing yours may help; a code reviewer needs the implementation.

Ask about important mistakes, alternative explanations, missed implications, and useful checks. Ask it to explain its reasoning and identify what it could not assess. A demonstrated error, a concern worth investigating, and a stylistic preference are different things. Do not require a quota of objections; finding no substantive problem is acceptable.

Treat the response as advice to examine, not a decision to obey. Check the important findings and explain which ones change the work. Two models agreeing does not make a claim true. Unresolved disagreement may be worth reporting rather than forcing into agreement.

Normally keep the consultation to review, leaving edits with the lead agent. Make a broader handover explicit when that would help. Avoid chains of reviewers calling each other. Continue the exchange while it resolves something useful, not just to obtain approval.

If the call is unavailable, give me a brief I can paste into the other tool and continue whatever can reasonably proceed. Do not change the setup just to use this snippet. Read the actual response before reporting a review, and tell me what it changed.
